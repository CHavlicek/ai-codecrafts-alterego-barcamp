# Feature Specification: Camera-Only Photo Intake — Tap the Circle, Take a Selfie

**Feature Branch**: `004-camera-only-capture`
**Created**: 2026-04-23
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: GitHub issue [#13 — Fix Take Photo Logic](https://github.com/squer-solutions/ai-codecrafts-alterego/issues/13). Exact request: *"Click on the 'Add Photo' Circle element should activate the device's camera and let user take a photo of themselves. This photo will then be used for image generation. 'Camera' button should be removed completely. 'Upload photo' should be removed completely."*

> **Relationship to earlier features.** This feature changes **only the photo-intake affordance** on the Setup tab introduced by 001 and restyled by 002. Every downstream behaviour — photo preview in the circle, client-side downscale, Generate gating, backend reduction before Gemini, poster rendering on the "Your Alter Ego" tab, no-persistence, accessibility baseline, fallback-on-failure — carries through unchanged. In particular, 002 FR-113 (which specified the circle + two side-by-side pill buttons "📷 Camera" and "🗂 Upload") is **superseded by this feature**: the circle becomes the sole interactive control for photo capture, and both pill buttons are removed.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-04-23

- Q: Where/how is the live camera feed shown between "user activates circle" and "user confirms photo"? → A: **Option A — inline, circle-as-viewfinder.** On browsers that support MediaDevices live capture (the happy path), the circle itself becomes the live viewfinder (the empty-state camera icon + label is replaced *in place* by the live video stream); a shutter / capture control appears directly **below** the circle, in the column space previously occupied by the removed 002 FR-113 pill buttons. No modal overlay, no separate preview panel, no lightbox, and no full-screen capture mode. The Setup tab's two-column layout stays intact during capture; the right-hand selections column remains visible and interactive. *(The separate question of what happens on browsers that do **not** expose MediaDevices — e.g. older mobile Safari — is resolved by a later clarification in this same session.)*
- Q: When the browser exposes no `getUserMedia` / MediaDevices capability at all (regardless of hardware or permission), what should the circle do? → A: **Option A — single capture surface; treat "no MediaDevices" as "no camera".** The FR-312 polite, non-blocking unavailability message is surfaced in the photo column, Generate stays disabled until a photo exists, and no fallback pathway (OS camera app via `<input capture="user">`, polyfill, or shim) is introduced. This keeps one capture surface and one test matrix across the POC; the affected population is narrow since the demo matrix targets modern evergreen browsers.
- Q: After the shutter fires, does the captured frame go straight into the circle, or does the user get a "Keep / Retake" step with the camera still running? → A: **Option B — still-preview with inline Keep / Retake; camera stream stays open across Retake.** Pressing the shutter captures the current video frame as a still shown in place of the live feed inside the circle; the camera stream stays open. Two inline controls appear: **Keep photo** commits the still as the captured photo and then stops + releases the camera stream; **Retake** discards the still and returns the circle to the live viewfinder, reusing the already-open stream (no second permission prompt, no second warm-up). The cancel affordance (Escape / inline cancel) remains available during the still-preview step and, when used, discards the still, stops the camera stream, and returns the circle to its prior state (empty or prior photo). The already-inherited "Retake / clear" button from 001 FR-002 is **different** — it clears the committed photo after Keep has been pressed — and is retained unchanged.
- Q: How should the circle viewfinder orient the front-camera video, and what does the still-preview + committed photo look like? → A: **Option A — mirror the live viewfinder, save the un-mirrored photo.** While the live preview is streaming inside the circle, the video MUST be rendered mirrored horizontally (the user's right hand appears on the right of the preview — the iOS Camera / FaceTime / macOS default). Once the shutter fires, the still-preview shown inside the circle MUST be rendered un-mirrored, and the photo eventually committed on Keep MUST also be un-mirrored. This preserves natural framing during capture while making the still-preview a true WYSIWYG preview of what Gemini will receive (text on clothing reads correctly, left/right cues in the prompt match the image). The rear-facing camera (FR-302 fallback), when used, MUST NOT be mirrored at any stage.
- **REVISION (post-implementation user testing, 2026-04-23)** — Q4's Option A was rejected in practice. Observed problem: even though the still-preview step correctly shows the un-mirrored image before Keep, the visual transition between the mirrored live viewfinder and the un-mirrored still-preview reads as a jump cut of the user's own face — "that's not what I was framing a moment ago". Users reported it as disorienting and a trust-eroding flip between "what I see" and "what gets saved". **New decision:** no mirroring at any stage of the front-camera path. The live viewfinder, the still-preview, and the committed bytes all render the raw un-mirrored camera orientation. Framing may feel slightly "backwards" (moving right-hand to the left edge of the viewfinder) — that is accepted as the lesser friction compared to the Option A flip. FR-302a is rewritten accordingly.
- Q: What geometry (aspect ratio and rough pixel budget) does the committed photo have? → A: **Option A — square (1:1), centred crop matching the visible circle, target roughly 1024×1024 pixels before the existing 001 downscale pipeline runs.** What the user frames inside the circle is what gets committed: the committed bytes correspond to the largest centred square that fits within the video frame, with no off-screen context leaking into the saved photo. The target ~1024×1024 pre-downscale size keeps parity with 003's Gemini reference-image convention (003 Assumptions §4) and makes the backend photo-reduction step (003 FR-206) a no-op in the typical case while staying well inside provider limits. Smaller-native-resolution cameras (older webcams below 1024×1024) are accepted as-is — the square crop happens at whatever resolution the camera exposes, and the downstream downscale / validation path (001 FR-001) handles the rest.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 — Tap the circle, take a selfie (Priority: P1)

A user lands on the Setup tab and sees the large circular photo area with "Add photo" shown inside it. They tap (or click, or activate via keyboard) the circle. The device's camera activates, they frame their face, take a photo, and confirm it. The photo appears inside the circle (cropped to a circle, matching the existing preview treatment), ready to be used for image generation. The user did not have to choose between "Camera" and "Upload photo" — the circle itself is the one-step affordance.

**Why this priority**: This is the entirety of the issue. Without it, the feature has not shipped.

**Independent Test**: On a laptop and on a mobile device, open the Setup tab, activate the circle, grant camera access, take a selfie, and observe the photo appear inside the circle. Then complete the rest of Setup, press Generate, and confirm a poster is produced with that photo as the face reference (delegating to 001/003 behaviour that is not under test here).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the Setup tab is open and the photo circle is empty, **When** the user activates the circle (tap, click, or Enter/Space on keyboard focus), **Then** the live camera preview renders *inside the circle* (the empty-state icon + label is replaced in place by the video stream) and a shutter button appears directly below the circle. Activating the shutter captures the current frame as a still shown in place of the live feed inside the circle; the shutter is replaced by two inline controls — "Keep photo" and "Retake". Activating "Keep photo" commits the still, stops and releases the camera stream, and the circle switches to the cropped-photo preview. Activating "Retake" instead discards the still and returns the circle to the live viewfinder (reusing the same open stream — no second permission prompt) so the user can try again. At no point during this flow does a modal overlay, a separate preview panel, a file-picker, or a "Camera" / "Upload photo" control appear.
2. **Given** a photo has already been captured and is shown in the circle, **When** the user activates the circle again (or uses the existing "Retake / clear" control), **Then** the camera re-activates, a fresh photo replaces the previous one, and the previous bytes are discarded from session state.
3. **Given** the user captures a photo and then presses Generate after completing the rest of Setup, **When** the Generate flow runs, **Then** the captured photo feeds the generation pipeline exactly as the previous upload-sourced photo did — no downstream behaviour regresses (poster rendering on the "Your Alter Ego" tab, backend photo reduction before Gemini per 003 FR-206, no-persistence posture per 001 FR-016 / 003 FR-215, fallback-on-failure per 003 FR-214).
4. **Given** the user is navigating by keyboard only, **When** they reach the photo circle via Tab, **Then** a visible focus indicator is shown on the circle, pressing Enter or Space activates the camera, and state changes (camera ready, photo captured, error) are announced to assistive technologies per 001 FR-022 / FR-023.

---

### User Story 2 — No more "Camera" or "Upload photo" pill buttons (Priority: P1)

Before this feature, the Setup tab's photo column rendered a circle **plus** two pill buttons underneath — "📷 Camera" (mobile-camera shortcut) and "🗂 Upload photo" (generic file picker). The issue is explicit that both buttons must be removed. After this feature, the photo column shows only the circle (and the existing "Retake / clear" control after a photo exists) — no pill buttons, no file-picker entry point.

**Why this priority**: This is a co-equal requirement of the same issue. The circle-as-trigger change and the pill-buttons-removed change are one atomic UX simplification — shipping one without the other leaves the UI incoherent (either two redundant affordances, or a non-functional circle).

**Independent Test**: Inspect the rendered Setup tab — on mobile viewports and desktop viewports — and confirm there are zero controls labelled "Camera" or "Upload photo" (including icon-only variants) anywhere in the photo column. A keyword search of the rendered DOM for those labels returns no hits.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the Setup tab is rendered on any supported viewport, **When** the user inspects the photo column, **Then** no button, link, or label containing the words "Camera" or "Upload" (or the emoji/icon equivalents 📷 / 🗂 used as pill-button labels in 002 FR-113) is present below the circle. The circle itself MAY continue to show a small camera icon inside its empty-state affordance — this is the circle's own label, not a separate Camera button.
2. **Given** the "Retake / clear" control defined by 001 (which appears after a photo exists), **When** the user captures a photo, **Then** that control still appears and still works — it is not one of the controls being removed.
3. **Given** no photo has yet been captured, **When** the user triggers a screen-reader walkthrough of the photo column, **Then** the only interactive controls announced are the photo circle itself (and, if applicable, the "Retake / clear" button when a photo exists).

---

### User Story 3 — Graceful handling when no camera is available (Priority: P2)

A user opens the Setup tab on a device without a working camera (desktop without a webcam, camera disabled in OS settings, or camera permission denied to the browser). They activate the circle, but the camera cannot start. Because upload is removed, they cannot fall back to picking a file — but the app must not strand them on a broken circle. The circle surfaces a non-blocking, accessible message explaining that camera access is required and how to resolve it; Generate remains disabled (per 001 FR-009) until a photo is captured. No spinner hangs forever, no silent failure, no uncaught error.

**Why this priority**: This is the degraded-path behaviour that falls out of removing upload. It is P2 rather than P1 because the baseline happy path (User Story 1) ships value on its own, but without P2 the feature has a user-hostile failure mode for a realistic subset of users (desktop demos on machines whose webcams are disabled, corporate policies blocking camera access, users who clicked "Block" the first time and forgot).

**Independent Test**: Simulate three conditions one at a time — (a) browser denies camera permission, (b) device exposes no camera at all, (c) camera permission is granted but the device is not exposing any usable camera stream — and confirm in each case that activating the circle produces a clear, polite, announced message in the photo column, that Generate remains disabled, and that a later retry (after granting permission or plugging in a camera) succeeds without a page reload.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the user activates the circle and the browser returns "permission denied" for camera access, **When** the denial is received, **Then** the photo column surfaces a message such as "Camera access is needed to take your photo — please allow camera access in your browser and try again", the circle remains in its empty state, Generate remains disabled, and the message is announced via an ARIA live region (per 001 FR-022 / FR-023).
2. **Given** the device exposes no camera at all (for example a desktop without a webcam), **When** the user activates the circle, **Then** a similar non-blocking message is shown ("A camera is required to take your photo — please connect a camera and try again") and the circle remains in its empty state.
3. **Given** camera access was initially denied and the user subsequently grants it (via browser settings), **When** they return to the tab and activate the circle again, **Then** the camera activates successfully and the capture flow proceeds as in User Story 1 — no page reload is required.
4. **Given** the camera activates but the user cancels or closes the capture UI without confirming a photo, **When** the cancel occurs, **Then** the circle remains in its prior state (empty if no prior photo, showing the previous photo if one existed), no error message is surfaced, and the user can re-activate the circle to try again.

### Edge Cases

- **Camera permission previously granted, then revoked between sessions**: Treated as "permission denied" on the next activation — surfaces the message from User Story 3 acceptance 1.
- **Multiple cameras (e.g. laptop internal webcam + external USB camera)**: The front-facing / user-facing camera is preferred. If only a rear-facing camera is available (mobile landscape-flipped tablet), it is used. Selecting between cameras is not part of this feature — the default the browser provides is accepted.
- **Mobile device rotation during capture**: Handled by the browser's capture UI; the app makes no additional commitment beyond what the platform provides.
- **User activates the circle repeatedly in rapid succession**: Only one capture session runs at a time. Subsequent activations while a session is active (live viewfinder open, or still-preview showing) are no-ops or re-focus the active capture UI.
- **User presses Retake repeatedly ("I blinked", "still blurry", "let me try once more")**: Each Retake reuses the already-open camera stream — no second `getUserMedia` prompt, no re-warm-up delay, no user-visible flicker beyond the still→live transition inside the circle. The camera stream is only stopped and released when the user presses Keep photo or Cancel.
- **Captured photo exceeds the 5 MB post-downscale limit enforced by 001**: Handled by the existing downscale + validation path (001 FR-001–003 behaviour). If downscale still yields > 5 MB, the existing rejection message is surfaced. This feature does not change those thresholds.
- **Keyboard activation on a non-button element**: Explicitly addressed by FR-310 — the circle is a real interactive button, not a decorative `<div>`, so Enter / Space activate it and screen readers announce it correctly.
- **Very old browser without MediaDevices support**: Treated the same as "no camera" — the User Story 3 message surfaces. No attempt is made to polyfill older browsers beyond what the Constitution's "modern evergreen browsers" posture already implies.
- **User takes a photo, Retake / clear is pressed, camera re-activates, cancel is pressed**: The photo is cleared (per 001 FR-002) and no new photo replaces it. The circle returns to the empty state. Generate remains disabled until a new photo is captured.

### Functional Requirements

**Circle becomes the single photo-intake affordance**

- **FR-301**: The large circular photo area in the Setup tab's photo column MUST be the single interactive control for photo capture. Activating it — by mouse click, touch tap, or keyboard (Enter or Space when focused) — MUST initiate the device's camera flow so the user can take a photo of themselves. **The live camera preview MUST render inline, inside the circle itself (the circle becomes the viewfinder), with the empty-state icon + label replaced in place by the live video stream. No modal overlay, dialog, lightbox, separate preview panel, or full-screen capture mode MUST be rendered. The Setup tab's two-column layout MUST remain intact while capture is open; the right-hand selections column MUST remain visible and interactive.**
- **FR-301a**: A visible **shutter / capture control** MUST appear directly below the circle while the live preview is active, in the column space previously occupied by the removed 002 FR-113 pill buttons. Activating the shutter MUST capture the current video frame as a **still image shown in place of the live feed inside the circle**; the camera stream MUST remain open at this point (i.e. the shutter does NOT by itself commit the photo or stop the stream). The shutter control MUST be keyboard-focusable with a visible focus indicator, have an accessible name describing its action (e.g. "Take photo"), and be announced as a button to assistive technologies. A visible **cancel** affordance MUST also be available while the preview is active (either a second inline button beside the shutter, or the Escape key — both are acceptable; at least one MUST be keyboard-operable). Cancel MUST stop and release the camera stream and return the circle to its prior state (empty or prior photo).
- **FR-301b**: Once the shutter has been fired and the still is shown in the circle, the system MUST render two inline controls beneath the circle (replacing the shutter while the still-preview is active): **"Keep photo"** and **"Retake"**. Keep photo MUST commit the still as the captured photo (at which point the camera stream MUST be stopped and released so the device's camera indicator turns off), and the circle MUST transition to the same cropped-photo preview used before this feature. Retake MUST discard the still and return the circle to the live viewfinder *reusing the already-open camera stream* — no second `getUserMedia` handshake, no repeat permission prompt, no second camera warm-up. Both controls MUST be keyboard-focusable with visible focus indicators, have accessible names, be announced as buttons to assistive technologies, and be placed so keyboard Tab order reaches Keep first and Retake second (or in whatever order follows the left-to-right visual placement chosen at planning time — the order MUST be consistent between visual and keyboard traversal). The cancel affordance from FR-301a MUST remain reachable while the still-preview is active; using cancel during the still-preview MUST discard the still, stop and release the camera stream, and return the circle to its prior state.
- **FR-302**: When the device exposes multiple cameras, the camera flow MUST prefer the front-facing / user-facing camera (the one pointed at the user). If only a non-user-facing camera is available, it MUST be used rather than blocking the flow.
- **FR-302a**: No stage of the capture surface MUST mirror the camera's video frame. The live viewfinder inside the circle, the still-preview shown after the shutter fires (per FR-301a / FR-301b), the bytes committed on Keep (per FR-301b), and every downstream consumer (downscale, Generate request, backend reduction, Gemini) MUST all render or transmit the raw un-mirrored frame exactly as the camera exposes it. The user's right hand appears on the left edge of the viewfinder and stays there through the still-preview and the committed photo — no CSS `transform: scaleX(-1)` on the `<video>` element, no mirror-option toggle on the canvas crop, no per-camera branching based on `facingMode`. This rule applies uniformly to the front-facing (default) and rear-facing (fallback per FR-302) camera paths: consistency between the viewfinder and the saved image is the load-bearing invariant, not conformance to the iOS Camera mirror convention.
- **FR-303**: Once a photo is captured and confirmed (per FR-301a / FR-301b — shutter fires, still-preview shown, Keep pressed), it MUST be shown cropped to the circle exactly as the 001/002 preview already renders it. The captured photo MUST then flow through the existing pipeline unchanged: client-side validation and downscale (001 FR-001–003), inclusion in the Generate request (002 FR-130, 003 FR-201), backend reduction before Gemini (003 FR-206–209), poster rendering on the "Your Alter Ego" tab.
- **FR-303a**: The **committed photo bytes** (what Keep produces, what is saved in session state, what feeds the Generate request) MUST correspond to the **largest centred square (1:1) crop** of the captured video frame — NOT the full rectangular video frame. The target pre-downscale resolution for that square crop MUST be approximately **1024×1024 pixels** when the camera exposes at least that resolution in each dimension. When the camera exposes a smaller native resolution (e.g. an older 720p webcam whose square crop is below 1024×1024), the square crop MUST be performed at whatever resolution is available and the downstream 001 FR-001 downscale / validation path applies unchanged. The square crop MUST be geometrically consistent with what the user saw inside the circle — no off-screen context beyond the circle's visible area MUST be included in the committed bytes. The square crop MUST apply to both the front-facing and rear-facing camera code paths (the latter per FR-302's fallback), and MUST occur before the FR-302a mirror/un-mirror transform is resolved for the committed output.
- **FR-304**: The user MUST be able to retake or replace the captured photo at any time before Generate. Re-activating the circle MUST initiate a fresh capture that, on confirmation, replaces the previous photo. The existing "Retake / clear" control from 001 MUST remain and MUST clear the current photo (returning the circle to its empty state) without triggering a new capture.
- **FR-305**: If the user cancels the capture flow — by activating the in-page cancel control or pressing Escape (per FR-301a), by navigating away from the Setup tab, or by any other browser/OS interruption that ends the live preview without the shutter firing — the circle MUST return to whatever state it held before the activation (empty if no previous photo existed, or displaying the prior photo if one did), the live video stream MUST be stopped and released (no "green camera light" left on), and no error message MUST be surfaced.

**Removed controls**

- **FR-306**: The "📷 Camera" pill button previously specified by 002 FR-113 MUST be removed entirely from the Setup tab. No button, link, menu item, or icon-only control offering a separate "Camera" entry point MUST remain below or beside the circle.
- **FR-307**: The "🗂 Upload photo" pill button previously specified by 002 FR-113 MUST be removed entirely from the Setup tab. No button, link, drag-and-drop zone, paste handler, or hidden file input surfacing a file-picker entry point MUST remain anywhere in the photo column.
- **FR-308**: No alternate photo-intake pathway (drag-and-drop onto the circle, paste from clipboard, URL-paste, "choose from gallery", etc.) MUST be exposed by this feature. The circle + camera is the only path. *(Later features may reintroduce alternate pathways if product decides, but this feature explicitly narrows the surface.)*

**Accessibility and labelling of the circle**

- **FR-309**: The circle MUST be a keyboard-focusable interactive control with an accessible name describing the action (for example "Take a photo of yourself") and with semantics that cause screen readers to announce it as a button. A visible focus indicator MUST be shown when the circle has keyboard focus, meeting 002 FR-112 contrast requirements.
- **FR-310**: The circle's empty-state visual (the camera icon and accompanying short text label inside the circle) MAY be updated in this feature to reflect the direct-camera interaction. The label MUST clearly communicate that activating the circle takes a photo (e.g. "Take photo" / "Tap to take photo" / "Take your photo"). Its exact wording is a plan-level UX detail; the constraint is that a user reading only the circle understands that activating it will open a camera, not a file picker.
- **FR-311**: State changes produced by circle activation (camera starting, photo captured, error) MUST be announced to assistive technologies via ARIA live regions per 001 FR-022. Error messages from FR-312/FR-313 MUST be programmatically associated with the circle per 001 FR-023.

**Graceful degradation when the camera is not available**

- **FR-312**: When the camera cannot be activated because (a) the browser returns "permission denied" to the camera request, (b) no camera device is exposed to the browser, (c) the browser does not support the MediaDevices / `getUserMedia` capability at all, or (d) the capability is nominally present but fails to return a usable video stream, the system MUST surface a **single, non-blocking, accessible message** within the photo column explaining that a camera is required. The message MUST be polite and MUST suggest the corrective action available to the user (grant camera permission, connect a camera, try a supported browser, try again). The circle MUST remain in its empty state; Generate MUST remain disabled until a photo is captured (per 001 FR-009). No fallback capture surface (OS camera app via the HTML `capture` attribute, polyfill, shim, or file picker) MUST be introduced for any of these cases — the app MUST ship exactly one capture surface (the inline circle viewfinder) with one degraded path (this message).
- **FR-313**: The exact copy of the camera-unavailable message MAY be tuned by implementation (it is a plan-level UX detail), but it MUST be **generic with respect to provider detail** — it MUST NOT expose raw browser error strings, stack traces, or permission-API names (e.g. `NotAllowedError`, `NotFoundError`) to the user. Reason codes MAY be logged for operator visibility but MUST NOT be surfaced in the UI. The message is informational, not a blocking modal.
- **FR-314**: If the user subsequently resolves the underlying condition (grants permission in browser settings, plugs in a webcam, etc.) and re-activates the circle, the camera flow MUST succeed on the next attempt without requiring a page reload or navigation. The app MUST NOT cache a prior "unavailable" verdict as permanent for the session.

**Downstream invariants preserved**

- **FR-315**: The captured photo bytes MUST be held only in browser memory (and, on the backend, in process memory for the duration of a Generate request per 001 FR-016 / 003 FR-215) — not persisted to disk, IndexedDB, localStorage, cookies, caches, or logs. The "no-persistence" posture of 001/003 applies identically to camera-captured photos as it did to uploaded photos.
- **FR-316**: The captured photo MUST go through the same client-side downscale + validation path (001 FR-001, max 5 MB post-downscale, JPEG/PNG produced by the camera pipeline) as the previous upload path did. The post-downscale payload MUST be compatible with the existing backend `GenerateRequest` contract (002 FR-130) with no contract change introduced by this feature.
- **FR-317**: All other Setup-tab behaviours (002 FR-101–112, 002 FR-114–124, 003 FR-201–219) MUST continue to work unchanged after this feature ships. No regression in tab switching, in Generate gating or re-run (002 FR-122–124), in Gemini integration or its fallback (003 FR-201–219), or in the "Your Alter Ego" tab's rendering (002 FR-107, 003 FR-205) is acceptable.

### Key Entities

- **Photo** — (inherited from 001) the user-supplied image used as the face reference for generation. Its *origin* changes from "uploaded file or device-camera capture through the `capture` attribute's fallback chain" to "live camera capture only". Everything else about the entity — content, format, size bounds, downscaling, transport, non-persistence — is unchanged.
- **CameraAvailabilityState** — a transient UI state on the Setup tab's photo column representing whether the camera can currently be activated. Values: *unchecked* (initial, before first activation), *available* (activated successfully at least once), *unavailable* (activation failed for one of the reasons enumerated in FR-312). Drives the empty-state label and the error message. Not persisted; resets on page reload.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-301**: On **100%** of the devices covered by the POC demo matrix (at minimum: a modern desktop/laptop browser with a working webcam, a modern mobile browser with a front-facing camera), activating the circle opens a camera flow and allows the user to take a photo within **one interaction** (tap/click/Enter — not a two-step "click circle, then click Camera" detour). *(Validates FR-301, FR-302.)*
- **SC-302**: After this feature ships, a text/DOM search of the rendered Setup tab for the strings "Camera" and "Upload" (case-insensitive, excluding the circle's own internal label and the accessible name of the circle) returns **zero** interactive controls in **100%** of supported viewports (desktop ≥ 1024 px, tablet, mobile). *(Validates FR-306, FR-307, FR-308.)*
- **SC-303**: In a fault-injection test matrix covering (a) camera permission denied, (b) no camera device present, (c) MediaDevices capability absent, **100%** of circle activations surface an accessible, announced message within the photo column within **2 seconds** of activation, with Generate remaining disabled and the page remaining fully usable for every other Setup control. No run hangs a spinner and no run surfaces a raw browser error string. *(Validates FR-312, FR-313, FR-314, User Story 3.)*
- **SC-304**: Keyboard-only users can complete photo capture end-to-end — reach the circle via Tab, activate it via Enter or Space, take and confirm a photo, and arrive at a state where Generate becomes enabled (all other Setup inputs being valid) — with a visible focus indicator throughout and every state change announced via an ARIA live region. Verified against **100%** of the acceptance scenarios under User Story 1. *(Validates FR-309, FR-311.)*
- **SC-305**: Automated accessibility scan (axe-core or equivalent) across the Setup tab in each of the three photo states — empty circle, captured-photo preview, camera-unavailable error — reports **zero** "serious" or "critical" violations. *(Validates FR-309, FR-311, FR-312.)*
- **SC-306**: End-to-end flow regression test — capture a photo via the circle, complete the rest of Setup, press Generate, observe a poster on the "Your Alter Ego" tab — succeeds in **100%** of attempts across the POC demo matrix, confirming that no downstream behaviour (client downscale, Generate request shape, Gemini integration and its fallback, poster rendering) has regressed relative to the pre-change baseline. *(Validates FR-303, FR-315, FR-316, FR-317.)*
- **SC-307**: In a **manual walkthrough of 5 consecutive captures** (take photo → Retake / clear → take another → Retake / clear → take a third), the circle correctly transitions between states in **100%** of transitions, no captured photo leaks from one capture into another, and session-state photo bytes are the latest capture's bytes at every point in the walkthrough. *(Validates FR-304, FR-305, FR-315.)*

## Assumptions

- **Capture mechanism — in-browser live camera capture via the MediaDevices API.** The previous implementation used a hidden HTML file input with `capture="user"`, which on mobile browsers opens the OS camera app and on desktop browsers falls back to a generic file picker. Because this feature explicitly removes the upload pathway, the desktop fallback would silently re-introduce an upload-style picker on the very devices where most POC demos run. The informed guess is therefore to use the browser's MediaDevices / `getUserMedia` camera capability on all platforms, delivering a uniform "live preview + shutter" experience on desktop and mobile alike. This is the same capability 001 FR-026 anticipated ("fully keyboard-operable in-browser camera capture"). **Preview placement and shutter placement are no longer plan-level open questions** — FR-301 / FR-301a, as clarified in the 2026-04-23 session, pin the preview *inside the circle* and the shutter *directly below the circle*. Remaining plan-level decisions are cosmetic (exact shutter icon / label wording, animation / transition timing, whether a subtle border treatment highlights the active viewfinder state).
- **Mobile browsers that do not expose a usable MediaDevices / `getUserMedia` stream** (older iOS Safari, restricted corporate webviews, embedded browsers): the clarified FR-312 degraded path applies — the photo column surfaces the polite "camera unavailable" message and Generate stays disabled. **No OS-camera-app fallback** (via `<input capture="user">`) is introduced — the app ships exactly one capture surface (the inline circle viewfinder). The affected population is narrow given the demo matrix targets modern evergreen browsers; shipping one surface rather than two is worth the narrow degraded path.
- **Only JPEG and PNG are accepted.** The existing downscale pipeline produces JPEG/PNG output (001 FR-001 validation rules). The live-camera capture path produces a JPEG blob from the captured frame, which feeds the same downscale + validation pathway. No new MIME types are introduced by this feature.
- **Captured photo resolution is sufficient for Gemini.** The live-camera capture path captures at the camera's native resolution (or the browser's exposed default) before the existing downscale step reduces it. Typical webcams (720p–1080p) and mobile front cameras (several megapixels) easily exceed the resolution Gemini needs for face anchoring (003 FR-207 / SC-203). No explicit minimum is pinned in this spec; SC-306 validates this end-to-end.
- **No in-app camera permission prompt is rendered by this feature.** The browser's native permission prompt is accepted as the consent mechanism. No additional opt-in dialogue, tooltip, or onboarding overlay is added by this feature; the existing Setup-tab copy plus the circle's label (FR-310) are sufficient.
- **No-persistence posture carries through.** Captured photo bytes are held only in in-memory objects (and the object URL created for preview). On Retake / clear, on tab navigation away, and on page reload, those bytes become unreachable — consistent with 001 FR-016/FR-024 and 003 FR-215.
- **Downstream contracts are untouched.** The `GenerateRequest` wire contract (002 FR-130), the backend photo-reduction step (003 FR-206–209), and the Gemini integration (003 FR-201–219) all see the same bytes they saw before, produced by the same downscale step they used before. This feature introduces no new backend work.
- **The "Retake / clear" control from 001 is retained and is not affected by the FR-306 / FR-307 removal.** The two pill buttons being removed are specifically the "📷 Camera" and "🗂 Upload photo" affordances introduced by 002 FR-113; "Retake / clear" has a different role (clear current selection) and remains.

- **Depends on**: 001-initial-poc (photo-intake behaviour contract — capture, replace, preview, downscale, validation, no-persistence, accessibility baseline), 002-sleek-tabbed-ui (photo column layout within the Setup tab, tabbed layout, dark-theme visuals), 003-gemini-image-generator (Gemini integration that consumes the captured photo).

- **Upstream constraint**: the Constitution's accessibility requirement (WCAG 2.1 AA, keyboard operability) and the modern-evergreen-browser target continue to apply.
- **Does not depend on**: any new backend work, any new API, any new third-party service, any new persistence layer, any new authentication, or any change to Gemini's wire contract.
